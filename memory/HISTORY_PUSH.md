## 0.3.2 → 0.3.5

Evolutions now follow the Pokémon you're actually looking at. Alolan
Vulpix correctly shows it needs an Ice Stone (not a Fire Stone), and
Galarian-only evolution lines like Farfetch'd → Sirfetch'd and Yamask →
Runerigus no longer show up on species that can't actually evolve that way.

A handful of alt-form bubbles that looked identical to the base Pokémon
(Deoxys's Attack/Defense/Speed Formes, Squawkabilly's Blue Plumage,
Tatsugiri's Droopy/Stretchy Forms) can now be tapped to view them, same as
every other form.

Tap either sprite on a Pokémon's detail page to see it enlarged. Paldea
Pokémon with no wild-encounter data now clearly say so ("needs source")
instead of implying they simply can't be found in the wild.
